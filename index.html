Frequently Asked Questions

What are your management fees?

Our management fees are competitive and transparent. Contact us for a customized quote based on your property type, location, and specific needs. We offer straightforward pricing with no hidden fees.

How do you screen tenants?

We conduct thorough background checks including credit history, criminal records, eviction history, employment verification, and rental references. Our screening process helps ensure quality tenants who pay on time and care for your property.

How quickly can you fill my vacancy?

Most properties are leased within 2-4 weeks, depending on market conditions, property condition, and competitive pricing. We use professional marketing strategies to minimize vacancy time and maximize your rental income.

Do I need to be local to use your services?

Not at all. Many of our clients are out-of-state or even international investors. Our comprehensive management approach and online owner portal make it easy to stay informed no matter where you are.

What happens if there's a maintenance emergency?

We provide 24/7 emergency maintenance support. Our network of vetted contractors responds quickly to urgent issues like plumbing leaks, HVAC failures, or electrical problems. You'll be notified and receive detailed reports of any emergency work.

How often will I receive financial reports?

You'll receive detailed monthly statements showing all income and expenses. Our owner portal provides 24/7 access to real-time financial information, maintenance records, and property documents.

Can I cancel the management agreement if needed?
